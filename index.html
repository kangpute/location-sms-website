Frequently Asked Questions

When do I need the Location SMS app?

Use it to quickly share your location by text message. The location coordinates can be used for navigation, helping others find you quickly. Common situations include contacting family or friends, meeting someone, picking someone up, ordering food, arranging a designated driver, traveling, getting around, asking for help, finding where you parked, and saving the locations of favorite food and scenic places.

What if the automatically detected address is inaccurate?

If the automatically detected address is your neighbor’s address, this may be a location error. Use “Manual Input” to enter and send the correct address once. The next time you use the app, this address will be given priority.

Does the recipient need to install the app?

No. The recipient receives a standard text message and can view the location using the map link in the message.

Does sending a text message cost anything?

The app does not charge a messaging fee, but your mobile carrier may charge according to your plan.

Why is location permission needed?

Location permission is used to get the current location you choose to share. You can manage this permission anytime in iPhone Settings.
