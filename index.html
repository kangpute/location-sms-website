Frequently Asked Questions

Does the recipient need to install the app?

No. The recipient receives a standard text message and can view the location using the map link in the message.

Does sending a text message cost anything?

The app does not charge a messaging fee, but your mobile carrier may charge according to your plan.

Why is location permission needed?

Location permission is used to get the current location you choose to share. You can manage this permission anytime in iPhone Settings.
